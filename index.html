About me

I work where agriculture meets markets, turning data into decisions that help farmers, firms and policymakers.

I am an Assistant Professor of Agribusiness at the Central University of Punjab, Bathinda, where I have taught in the M.B.A. (Agribusiness) programme since January 2020. My Ph.D. and M.B.A. in Agribusiness are from the Institute of Agri Business Management, Swami Keshwanand Rajasthan Agricultural University, Bikaner, built on a B.Sc. (Hons) in Horticulture and an ICAR National Talent Scholarship.

My research spans agricultural price modelling and forecasting, agripreneurship, climate-resilient agribusiness models, consumer behaviour in food systems and the socioeconomic impact of smart farming. I lead the AgriBusiness Intelligence Lab, an international group of Ph.D. scholars from India, Afghanistan and Ghana, and I have guided many M.B.A. dissertations along the way.

Before academia I worked in fertiliser sales and marketing and as a Senior Research Fellow on a national market-intelligence project at ICAR-NIAP. That mix of field, market and research experience shapes how I teach and what I study.

About my lab

AgriBusiness Intelligence Lab (ABI Lab)

Central University of Punjab · Department of Applied Agriculture

The AgriBusiness Intelligence Lab studies agricultural markets, supply chains, consumers and entrepreneurship. Using analytics, it helps farmers, firms and policymakers make decisions on better evidence.

The lab is an international group: its Ph.D. scholars come from India, Afghanistan and Ghana and are supported by Government of India (NFST) and ICCR fellowships.
